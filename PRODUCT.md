# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TokPeek is for macOS developers who use AI coding agents such as Claude Code,
Codex, Cursor, Gemini, and other clients supported by Tokscale. They need a
fast, trustworthy view of local token consumption while working, without
leaving their current task or sending session contents to another service.

## Product Purpose

TokPeek turns Tokscale Core's local usage reports into a polished native macOS
menu bar experience. Its primary job is to answer, at a glance, how many tokens
were used, what they cost, how usage is changing, and which clients contributed.
Success means the menu bar remains useful in a two-second glance while richer
detail and preferences stay one click away.

## Brand Personality

Native, restrained, refined. TokPeek should feel calm and dependable rather
than flashy, with the familiarity and interaction quality of established macOS
productivity tools.

## Anti-references

TokPeek should not resemble a dense web analytics dashboard, a neon gaming
monitor, a crypto price ticker, or a decorative glassmorphism concept. It
should not imitate TokenBar's visual identity or use excessive cards, gradients,
badges, or motion to make ordinary statistics appear more important.

## Design Principles

1. Glance first: the most important current usage signal is understandable in
   two seconds.
2. Native by default: use standard macOS controls, terminology, behavior, and
   system materials wherever they serve the task.
3. Progressive detail: summary, trend, breakdown, and configuration appear in
   that order without overwhelming the menu panel.
4. Local trust: make local processing and data-source behavior explicit and
   never imply that session content is uploaded.
5. Calm precision: use restrained color, monospaced numerals, clear units, and
   predictable interactions instead of decorative urgency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support system light and dark appearances, VoiceOver labels, keyboard
navigation, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, color-blind-safe chart
encoding, and Reduce Motion. Text and controls must remain legible at larger
accessibility sizes without clipping important values.
